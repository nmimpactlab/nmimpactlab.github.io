Set 2 of 3 — the employed workforce. Of the 82,266 current license holders in Set 1, 40,148 (49%) are employed in New Mexico public education this year — PED names an employer for them, or they hold an active assignment. This set re-runs Set 1 on just those people. Where they work comes in the third set.

The shape flips from Set 1: among the employed, Teacher-only is the largest group. Support-only licenses (substitutes, aides) are employed at the lowest rate, so they fall to second.

Specialty Area, employed, by subject

The 3,742 employed holders of a Specialty Area license — licensed to teach one subject across every grade. A specialist may hold several subjects, so these counts aren’t additive.

Teacher endorsements, employed, by subject

Each bar counts endorsements, not teachers. Of the 25,857 employed teachers, 19,773 hold at least one subject endorsement; 6,084 hold none (no endorsement is required at the elementary / early-childhood levels). One teacher can hold several, so subjects add to more than the number of teachers.
