Of the licensed, how many are not employed?

Each license type, split into employed (teal) and not currently employed in NM public education (clay). Overall, 42,118 of the 82,266 licensed (51%) are not employed in a New Mexico public school this year. Support-only licenses (substitutes, aides) account for most of the gap; teacher-plus-support holders are employed at the highest rate.
