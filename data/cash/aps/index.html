APS cash disbursements, 2010–2026

Every cash disbursement Albuquerque Public Schools published in its board documents — July 2010 through June 2026 — compiled into one searchable register. Search a vendor, then click the row to see each individual payment with a pointer to the source document.

This register shows payment facts as the district published them: payee, date, amount, check/voucher number, and (from July 2025) the district's own expense coding. Remit city and address details are intentionally omitted. 185 of 192 months are present; the district never published registers for 7 months (May–Jul 2019, Mar & Jul 2020, Apr & Jul 2021) — those are obtainable only by records request.
