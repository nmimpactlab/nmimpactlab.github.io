Methodology & sourcing

How we collect, verify, and publish, and what we won't post.

Everything on this site is built from public records: documents and data that New Mexico law already makes available to anyone. What follows is how that gets done. For the specifics of any one dataset – where it came from, what period it really covers, and what is missing from it – see sources & gaps.

Where the data comes from

Four state systems carry most of what is here: nmlegis.gov for bill histories, sponsors, committee actions and roll-call vote sheets; the Secretary of State's lobbyist registration system; the Secretary of State's campaign-finance disclosure system; and the Public Education Department's published extracts and Sunshine Portal downloads. Court records are in planning. Sources & gaps names the files behind each dataset and says which ones link back to an individual filing and which ones cannot.

How we collect it

Collection and processing run on scripts written for this project, one per dataset, each rerunnable from the original files. Where a record is only on a website we retrieve it programmatically, at a polite rate, and only where the host permits automated access. Two New Mexico sources post a blanket no-automated-access rule and are therefore not collected from at all; sources & gaps names them. We keep a copy of every original file, so any published figure can be traced back to the record it came from.

How we verify it

Names, dates and amounts have to be made consistent before anything can be counted, because the state's systems spell the same organization several ways and rarely share an identifier across systems. We keep the filed value next to the cleaned one, so the cleaning can be checked rather than taken on trust. Every cleaning rule that could change a published number is written down, with what it costs, on sources & gaps.

What we publish, and what we don't

  • We publish facts drawn from public records, with sources, and let readers draw their own conclusions.
  • We focus on public officials and institutions acting in their official capacity.
  • We do not publish private personal information (such as home addresses, personal contact details, or other sensitive identifiers) even when it appears in a record.
  • We correct errors promptly. See Contact.
This project is not affiliated with any campaign, party, agency, or single institution. Our aim is a durable, statewide public record, not any one target.
