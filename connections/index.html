Pick two or more New Mexico people or organizations and see how they connect across the public record — a shared lobbyist, a shared campaign donor or recipient committee, or a shared registered contact point.

What a connection means. Links here come only from public disclosures: a registered lobbyist and the organizations they represent, an organizational campaign donor and the committees it funds, and organizations that share a registered contact point. Individuals other than registered lobbyists are not included. A shared lobbyist, donor, or registered agent shows a relationship — not coordination or wrongdoing. Verify against the source before drawing any conclusion.
