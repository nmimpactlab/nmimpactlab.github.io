New Mexico's civic-board network

Who serves on the boards of New Mexico's chambers of commerce, civic nonprofits, and education advocacy groups — and the people who sit on more than one. Search a board or a person to isolate them and their direct connections, then click any connection to open up its connections, one step at a time. Export whatever you trace.

Coverage: a documented snapshot, not a census. Seats are compiled from organizations' own published board rosters, IRS filings, funder grant disclosures, and campaign-finance reports, as collected in mid-2026. Rosters change and no organization's list here is guaranteed complete — absence from this map means only that a seat isn't in the compiled records yet. Use the legend to filter by type.
