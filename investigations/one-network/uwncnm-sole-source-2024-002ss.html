The sole source

A $200,000 no-bid agreement for Ford Next Generation Learning services, requested by the superintendent, with United Way of North Central New Mexico designated the “exclusive coordinating entity” — while the superintendent co-chairs a UWNCNM initiative. Payments continued months past the agreement's stated term.

The timeline

  1. Feb 18, 2025
    Sole-source determination signed.

    Procurement's determination memo justifies a no-bid award under NMSA 1978 § 13-1-126: “The United Way serves as the exclusive coordinating entity for Ford NGL services in the region.” A same-dated attachment describes a public-private partnership in which APS contributes $200,000 and UWNCNM contributes $200,000 toward the Ford NGL project.

    Determination packet (10 pp) (archived on web.archive.org)
  2. Mar 2025
    Posting and the 30-day rule.

    The district's own form states: “A sole source determination is not effective until the sole source request for determination has been posted for thirty (30) calendar days without challenge.” The archived posting shows 2024-002ss on aps.edu by March 19, 2025 — while the request form states a term beginning in March 2025 and ending June 30, 2025.

    Archived aps.edu posting (archived on web.archive.org)
  3. Apr 3 & Jun 19, 2025
    Two payments of $98,930 each.

    Together $197,860 — effectively the $200,000 contribution — both within the stated term.

    Payment register (PDF)
  4. Dec 9, 2025 – May 13, 2026
    Seven further payments after the stated term.

    Approximately $46K across seven voucher payments, months after June 30, 2025. The sole-source form states: “Renewal of Sole Source Procurement must be re-submitted for new term.” Whether a renewal was posted is not in the compiled record.

    Payment register (PDF)

What the record shows — and doesn't

Sole-source procurement is lawful when only one vendor can provide a service, and the determination memo makes that case on Ford NGL's proprietary framework. What the record shows alongside it: the request came from the superintendent's own office (her name and email are on the form); the compiled board-roster data places her as co-chair of UWNCNM's Rising Together initiative; and payments continued for months past the agreement's stated term, though the form requires resubmission for a new term. Whether a renewal exists is an open question the compiled record cannot answer — a records request could.
