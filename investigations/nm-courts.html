Court & Financial Records

Courts · Planned

New Mexico's courts publish case records one docket at a time. Nothing in that record can be counted as it stands: there is no way to ask how long a case type takes, or how often the same party appears, without assembling the record yourself first. That is what this project would do.

What it will cover

  • Publicly available court records and dockets.
  • Associated financial data, where public.
  • One row per case, so that cases can be counted.

Sources

Records will come from New Mexico's public court systems. The access question comes first here, ahead of any analysis: a good deal of the docket record sits behind an account, and what sits behind an account is not collectable.

Status: In planning. The open question is which parts of the docket record a member of the public can reach without an account, because that line decides what the dataset can be. Interested or have a lead? research@nmimpactlab.org.
